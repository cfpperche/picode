### Changed
- **One scope vocabulary in setup tab links.** Packages, Connectors, Skills, Memory, Settings, Keyboard and Models now all put their scope in the address the same way — `?scope=global`, `?scope=workspace` or `?scope=agent` — and moving between these tabs keeps the scope you chose. Older links (`?scope=project`, `?scope=user`, `?scope=machine`, `?layer=…`) still work and are rewritten.
