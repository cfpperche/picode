### Changed

- **Scrollbars stay out of sight until you point at them.** Every scrolling
  area now hides its scrollbar while the mouse is elsewhere, the way the
  sidebar already did: pointing at the area, focusing it from the keyboard
  or dragging the bar brings it back, with no shift in the layout. Touch
  screens keep their bars drawn, and the Instructions table that scrolls
  sideways keeps its bar visible so its hidden columns stay obvious.
