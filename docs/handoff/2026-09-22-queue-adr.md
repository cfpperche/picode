# 2026-09-22 — queue-adr: the D3 boundary, written down and waiting for a yes
The owner's answers from the session are inside ADR-0182 rather than in prose: the queue serves **any project that wants it**, and PiCode works with and without it (the rule D2's removal taught); the queue's *configuration* follows workspace → machine; and identity stays a **launch** value, never a scope value — the Hermes/Muse measurement of 2026-09-21 is cited as the reason a scope value cannot stand in for it.
The decision it asks for: entries are durable intents stored beside the delivery they name (the D1a pattern — migration, optimistic version, `delivery.changed` in the same transaction); one integration operation per repository through the land that exists, which keeps writing its receipts so the queue's evidence is the evidence the gates already produce; eligibility bound to the reviewed revision and expiring with it; agents may enqueue and withdraw their own delivery, only the owner orders or authorizes; unknown stays unknown after a restart, and a duplicate request replays its receipt.
Alternatives are recorded with the reason each lost — hand-run integration, an in-memory queue, agent-held execution authority, and identity-as-scope-value.
Status is `proposed`: the slice starts when the owner accepts it (or says which clause to change). Nothing else in the delivery topic waits on a decision.
